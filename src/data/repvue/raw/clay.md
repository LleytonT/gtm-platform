Clay is an AI-driven go-to-market (GTM) development platform that equips growth and sales teams with customer data and automated workflows. The company provides access to over 150 premium data providers, AI research agents, and intent signals to help businesses find, contact, and close customers more effectively. Clay's platform combines first-party data, intent data, and third-party data from multiple sources in one place, enabling GTM teams to conduct deeper customer research and automate outreach without writing code.

Founded in 2017 by Kareem Amin and Nicolae Rusan (with Varun Anand joining as co-founder in 2021), Clay started as an enhanced spreadsheet tool connecting external databases and evolved into a comprehensive sales intelligence and automation platform. The company spent six years refining its product before experiencing explosive growth starting in 2022. Clay's platform enables users to aggregate data from 150+ sources, build AI-powered workflows with Claygent, create custom signals to track buyer intent, and automate personalized outreach at scale.
